Italy Auction Radar, Issue 2

Eight more Italian court auction lots in plain English, checked against the sale notices and appraisals on the official Portale delle Vendite Pubbliche. Issue 1 is here.

How to read the discountThe discount is measured against the court's own estimate, which already includes deductions. Some lots below are a second round, so the start price has already fallen once. Non-EU buyers may need a reciprocity check, and some sales are held in person, in Italian.
