Lot Report: flat in Settala (Milan)

Sample of a single-lot report. Everything is taken from the sale notice and the court appraisal of procedure 637/2025, Tribunal of Milan. Source documents: official listing.

1. Verdict in short

  • Plain case. Clean papers, no mortgage or court claims that stay with the buyer, small works only. The main risk is the occupant, who has no right to stay but still has to be removed.
  • Price. Start price €69,000, about €1,190 per m². The appraiser's market average for the area is €1,300 per m².
  • Not covered by the court estimate: taxes, transfer costs, half of the court agent's fee, any condominium debts, removal of the occupant if you waive the custodian's help.

4. Risks, checked in the documents

Occupied without a titleThe notice says the flat is occupied without a legal title. The custodian clears it, on the timing set by the judge, unless the buyer asks to do it alone. The appraisal found no registered lease from the owner.
Condominium debtsArrears were about €1,250 at 31 Dec 2025 and ordinary costs about €1,400 a year. By law the buyer is jointly liable for the year of the sale and the year before, so ask the administrator for current figures before bidding.
No certificatesThe appraisal lists no energy certificate, no installation certificates and no structural safety certificate. Factor in the cost of the energy certificate and of checking the installations.
Small worksA small plasterboard storage closet in the living room has to be removed. The appraisal puts the regularisation cost at €0 and calls the flat essentially compliant.
ConditionNo lift, and the appraisal rates the building and the flat as poor. The flat has electrical, plumbing and heating installations.

5. What stays and what goes

  • Stays with the buyer: nothing registered against the flat. The appraisal found no court claims, criminal measures or building restrictions.
  • Cleared by the court: mortgages and seizures, at the procedure's cost.
  • The buyer pays: taxes, transfer costs and half of the court agent's fee for the transfer. The notice does not state the amounts.

7. For a foreign buyer

  • You need an Italian tax code before paying the balance.
  • The offer must include the buyer's ID and tax code, and for a foreign citizen a copy of a valid residence permit, according to the notice. Check with an Italian lawyer what applies to your nationality.
  • Offers go by certified e-mail through the Ministry of Justice portal, and a lawyer can bid for you with a notarised power of attorney, including for a person to be named later.

8. Checklist before you bid

  1. Read the full appraisal and notice on the portal and ask a lawyer to review them.
  2. Ask the custodian to show the flat and ask how long clearing the occupant is expected to take.
  3. Get current condominium arrears from the administrator.
  4. Price the energy certificate, the installation checks and the closet removal.
  5. Get an Italian tax code and set up the deposit transfer so it is credited in time.
  6. Decide your maximum price including taxes and costs, not only the bid.
