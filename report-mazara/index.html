Lot Report: house with pool in Mazara del Vallo (Sicily)

Sample of a single-lot report on a risky lot. Everything is taken from the sale notice and the court appraisal of procedure 83/2025, Tribunal of Marsala. Source documents: official listing. A plainer lot is here.

1. Verdict in short

  • A cheap price with a real legal problem. Part of the property was built without a permit and the appraisal says some of it may not be legalisable. The town could order demolition.
  • Not habitable on paper. The appraisal says the house has no occupancy certificate.
  • Price. Start price €97,364.56, about €353 per m² of the 276 m² the appraiser counts. The appraiser values the property at €630 per m² before deductions.
  • The appraiser's own arithmetic does not add up. See the flag below.

4. What stays and what goes

  • Cleared by the court: a voluntary mortgage of €200,000 registered in 2024 and the seizure of 2025, at the procedure's cost.
  • Stays with the buyer: the property is sold as seen, with its planning problems. The notice says defects, planning issues and missing works do not reduce the price.
  • Special amnesty window: where the law allows, the buyer can file a retroactive permit within 120 days of notification of the transfer decree.

6. For a foreign buyer

  • Offers are submitted through the court online platform, and a lawyer can bid for you, including for a person to be named later. Ask the court agent which identity and tax documents are required for a foreign buyer.
  • Reciprocity is not discussed in the notice; see the flag above.
  • Check with a local planning professional whether the detached block can be legalised before you decide your maximum price.

7. Checklist before you bid

  1. Read both versions of the appraisal and the notice, and ask a lawyer to review them.
  2. Ask a Sicilian technician to check at the town hall what can be legalised and what must be demolished.
  3. Ask the court agent about the €2,700 difference in the final value.
  4. Ask for a viewing and check who still lives there and how the custodian plans to free the house.
  5. Price taxes, transfer costs, the €18,000 for the works, and a margin for demolition.
  6. Plan the 20% deposit so it is credited by 23:59 the day before the sale.
