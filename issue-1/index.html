Italy Auction Radar, Issue 1

Seven Italian court auction lots in plain English, checked against the sale notices and appraisals on the official Portale delle Vendite Pubbliche. Each card shows the price, the court estimate and what can go wrong.

Before you readA big discount to the court estimate is not always a bargain: after failed rounds the start price can fall far below it. Non-EU buyers may also need a reciprocity check, and some sales are held in person at the court, in Italian.
